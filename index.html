Welcome to OLAM

Where the warmth of home meets the beauty of Kerala.

Tucked away amidst the tropical greenery of Kerala, OLAM by Plasseril is a place to slow down, breathe deeply and simply feel at home.

Created with warmth, comfort and genuine hospitality at heart, OLAM welcomes you to experience peaceful mornings, quiet evenings and the simple beauty of staying close to nature.

The OLAM Experience

Stay a while. Feel at home.

At OLAM, we believe that the most memorable journeys are often found in the simplest moments.

A quiet morning with the sunlight filtering through the trees. An evening spent surrounded by greenery. A comfortable room to return to after a day of exploring.

Whether you are travelling with family, friends, or simply looking for a peaceful escape, OLAM is a place where you can slow down and reconnect.
